# Bench: fewer deterministic rules (corrections.py, gate text rules), 2026-11-13

Branch `w4-rules`, client only. Set `eval/dev-v1` (122 clips), config
`docs/bench/eval-baseline/baseline.toml`, own server pair: `skriv-server --backend parakeet_mlx` on
127.0.0.1:50081, S1-mini (`s1-mini-q4_k_m`, flags of `scripts/llama-up`) on 127.0.0.1:8081. Motivation:
the rules study (`/tmp/hark-rules-study/REPORT.md`) found that deterministic self-correction resolution changes
no final text, and that the anchor gate's value is its confidence transfer, not its text decisions.

## Changes

- `corrections.py` (194 lines), `cleanup.resolve_corrections` and its pipeline step are gone. S1-mini applies
  spoken self-corrections; `fidelity.py` still accepts a rewrite that drops a cue-bounded retraction.
- `gate.py` 441 → 95 lines. It aligns the two decodes (difflib, as before) and gives every biased word its anchor
  confidence (a differing region: the anchor's least confident word there; nothing heard: its own). Nine of the
  ten revert rules are gone (`inserted`, `outside_term`, `in_anchor`, `negation`, `number`, `inflection`,
  `not_close`, `word_dropped`, `anchor_confident`), with the per-region decision records, the bias-hit regions,
  the phonetic key, and `gate.max_distance` / `gate.confident_anchor`. `caps_oov` stays (see below). History:
  `gate = {status, anchor_text, reverted}`; the eval report loses `gated` and `corrections`.

Raw, canonical and final texts are identical on all 122 clips. Uncleaned texts differ on 7 clips only, the
correction clips syn-066 … syn-072: they used to be resolved before the model and now reach it as spoken
("Set the timeout to 3 seconds, sorry 5 seconds."), and S1-mini writes the same final in every one
("Set the timeout to 5 seconds.", "Put it in server/src.", "Call it fetch_row_batch.", "Run it on the CPU
foulback.", "Write it in Rust.", "Add it to docs/eval.md.", "Make the limit 20 megabytes."). No clip changed
final text, so there is nothing new to review or accept.

Before, the gate reverted text on two clips: syn-047 (`in_anchor`: "localhost colon 8000") and syn-008
(`caps_oov`). syn-047's canonical text is the same without the revert (canonicalization writes `localhost:8000`
from the biased words too).

## The one rule kept: `caps_oov`

A first after-run removed every text rule. Everything above held except syn-008: "handle MT transcripts" instead
of the accepted "handle empty transcripts" (accepted 76 → 75; not a mechanical failure). That is the measurement the
task asked for, so `caps_oov` stays, the gate's one text rule (one all-caps non-word, no shortlist word, where the
anchor heard one English word). It still has only that one benefiting clip in dev-v1, and it would also reject a
real out-of-list acronym heard by the anchor as a word (`LLM` / `well`): it should go once decoding or the model
handles syn-008.

## Risks

- With cleanup off, timed out or rejected, spoken self-corrections now stay in the pasted text (before, the
  deterministic pass resolved the seven shapes above). That keeps every word said rather than guessing, and the
  ablation `no-cleanup.toml` now measures it. Not run here.
- dev-v1's correction clips are synthetic and few (7); real speech has none.
